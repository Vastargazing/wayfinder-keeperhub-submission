# Product

A user starts a strategy and sees the confirmed state of its monetary operations.
When execution stops, the console distinguishes a known result from uncertainty
and names the evidence needed to proceed. It does not offer resend actions.

The real SDK, executor, journal, durable driver and console are included. The
supported demonstration is offline/model; see [reproduction](reproduction.md).
Historical mint consumption is documented separately in the
[evidence index](../evidence/INDEX.md). A new live Aave cycle is unverified.
